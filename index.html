PDF Sharing & My Documents

1. Goal and scope

Enable caregivers to revisit the most recently saved output of each Careverse tool, download it, and email it to clinicians or other caregivers without requiring recipients to create a Careverse account.

IN SCOPE Naming PDF; save to My Documents; override outdated PDF with most-recent data; download; direct email sharing; table display.

OUT OF SCOPE Archive/version history; recipient accounts; secure sharing links; PDF access codes; automatic clinician-directory integration; scheduled expiry.

5. Privacy and security concerns

Direct PDF email is an irreversible disclosure. A valid email address is not proof of the intended clinician's identity. Confirm recipient, minimise content in email subject/body, use encrypted transport and vetted email provider, restrict access to saved responses, and define breach-response ownership. Patient/caregiver authorisation and Malaysian data-protection review remain required.
